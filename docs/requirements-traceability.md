# Requirements traceability

Source: `Requirements.pdf` sections 11-13. Status vocabulary: Planned, In progress, Done. Paths under `src/app` and `src/server/admin` are the planned locations and may change; verify when pages land.

Phase key: 1 = MVP, 1.5 = Enhanced, 2 = Future (checkout, payments, wishlist, Urdu, analytics). Phases follow the table in section 13.3.

Ambiguity: section 13.3 lists CS-09 in Phase 1 (via the range CS-01 to CS-12) and again in Phase 1.5. It is recorded as "1 / 1.5" and built with Phase 1 (badges are also required by AS-04). Also, Phase 2 includes "Full checkout + payment gateway" (see ADR 0011), which has no story ID.
